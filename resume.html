Academic & Laboratory Investigations

Physics Extended Essay — Full-Wave Bridge Rectifier & Capacitive Smoothing

Experimental Electronics & Circuit Analysis
Extended Essay

Research Question: How does the capacitance of the capacitor influence the average DC output voltage of a full-wave bridge rectifier?

  • Investigated capacitor smoothing dynamics in a full-wave bridge rectifier circuit under AC excitation.
  • Engineered an experimental testbench driven by a 5 VAC, 50 Hz input using discrete 1N4007 power diodes.
  • Evaluated filter capacitors ranging from 10 µF to 50 µF to observe exponential RC discharge characteristics and DC offset stabilization.
  • Captured and analyzed peak-to-peak ripple voltage and mean output voltage across the resistive load using an analog oscilloscope.
  • Correlated empirical observations directly with theoretical rectifier transfer functions and mathematical capacitor-filter models.
Electronics Oscilloscope 1N4007 Diodes Capacitor Filtering RC Circuit Theory

Chemistry HL Internal Assessment — Ionic Radius & Enthalpy of Dissolution

Inorganic Calorimetry & Thermochemical Modeling
HL Internal Assessment

Research Focus: Relationship between ionic radius and enthalpy of dissolution of divalent metal chlorides.

  • Investigated dissolution thermodynamics across hydrated divalent metal salts: MgCl₂, ZnCl₂, CoCl₂, NiCl₂, and CuCl₂.
  • Conducted high-precision calorimetric temperature measurements to evaluate net enthalpy of solution ($\Delta H_{sol}$).
  • Calculated lattice enthalpies and hydration energies relative to cation crystal ionic radii.
  • Applied quantitative data processing, propagated systematic and random uncertainties, and analyzed transition metal d-orbital influences.
Calorimetry Enthalpy of Dissolution Inorganic Chemistry Error Propagation

Mathematics AA HL Internal Assessment — RC Circuit Calculus Modeling

Calculus & Differential Equations in Transient Electrical Behavior
HL Internal Assessment

Research Focus: Mathematical modeling and calculus formulation of RC circuit charging and discharging behavior.

  • Derived first-order linear differential equations governing transient voltage and current in series resistor-capacitor networks.
  • Modeled exponential charging ($V(t) = V_0(1 - e^{-t/RC})$) and discharging curves using integration techniques and boundary conditions.
  • Correlated theoretical analytical models with practical time constant ($\tau = RC$) behavior in real electronic components.
Differential Equations Calculus Modeling Exponential Functions Circuit Analysis
